<h1>How to assembly everything.</h1>
Sorry, but I won't be measuring every dang screw I've used for this project. If you really want to print it (it uses about 1.2kg for the black parts alone!), modify the model to fit your screws or just try to get the dimensions from the photo bellow:

<img width="2000" height="auto" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5136718-ac2f-45ce-b770-64af045403b2" />

Now that you've got your screws and parts printed, put some hot glue or silicone in the channels on the clamp. They make sure the arm won't come off the windowsill and scratch it.

<img width="1000" height="auto" alt="Ramie_monitora_2026-Oct-18_07-36-23PM-000_CustomizedView19883709315" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b6a86bc4-7553-4088-94a9-8c443034cd95" />


Assemble the clamp by putting one part of the hinge into the plate with 28 screws and then screw in the top-bottom plate to it.

<img width="1000" height="auto" alt="Ramie_monitora_2026-Oct-18_07-58-44PM-000_CustomizedView38029924027" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6af57616-0b93-4410-a65c-5ee506ede9c8" />

Screw in the 3 screws on top and bottom
<img width="1000" height="auto" alt="Ramie_monitora_2026-Oct-17_08-11-03PM-000_CustomizedView26192489886" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/14cd5d06-366a-4228-9323-ca7d901cfb44" />



Put in six M6 nuts into the bottom panel and screw in your M6 bolts (I'm using the ones that are 50mm in length). Put that thing with glue/silicone on top of your screws and the clamp is done!



Now let's move on to the vesa mount. Put the hinge part to the plate with vesa mount and screw in the two screws to secure it. 

<img width="725" height="388" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/55d9ebaa-3566-4fe7-88b9-fdbdbb698217" />

Screw in 3 screws on top and bottom, too

<img width="1000" height="auto" alt="Ramie_monitora_2026-Oct-17_08-10-09PM-000_CustomizedView12896116754" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2b2c5943-63e1-413f-b228-508d5151b0b5" />

I expect you won't be using the speaker mounts since they were designed for some random old TV speakers I had, but if you do, then screw them in with 3 screws on both sides

<img width="639" height="487" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/50fec172-0548-486b-a35d-9829820e811c" />


Now let's add the cable channels! Just slide the orange part onto the black one and screw it in with 3 screws, do the same thing for the other part.

<img width="1920" height="722" alt="Ramie_monitora_2026-Oct-18_07-13-36PM-000_CustomizedView4120106074" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a77c2092-a86b-42be-a727-38bbaa880c6a" />

<h2>Final assembly</h2>

Now that everything is done, put everything together like on the render and use some M6 bolts as hinge pins. I would recommend to use one washer on the bottom and two nuts to create a jam nut. Don't screw them in too much, leave some room to avoid friction! I'm using the ones that are 100mm in length.

The only thing left to do is to screw in the Benchy, and you're done!
